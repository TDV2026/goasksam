About GoAskSam

What this is

GoAskSam tells you where to sell your enthusiast car and why. It reads real auction sale records and turns them into one clear recommendation: the platform where cars like yours have actually been selling best, with the evidence behind it. No estimates. No valuations. No guesswork.

Who's behind it

GoAskSam is built by Sam Gold, also behind The Daily Vroom, the collector car newsletter that has tracked this market every day for years. GoAskSam is the seller side of that work: the same daily attention to real results, pointed at one question, which is where you should sell your car.

How it works

Sam pulls comparable sales for your exact car from the auction platforms we track, looks at how each platform has performed for that kind of vehicle, and factors in timing. The data does the heavy lifting; our own rules and methodology turn it into a recommendation with honest confidence. When the exact match is thin, Sam widens the read a step at a time and says so, rather than dressing up a guess as a certainty.

What it will never do

Sam never invents a number. Every figure you see comes from a real sale in our records. GoAskSam never puts a valuation on your car, never sends your details out to a list of buyers, and never claims a platform gets you more money than the evidence actually shows. When the read is less certain, we tell you plainly.

Where we stand

We are independent and data first. Nobody pays to be recommended. The recommendation follows the sales record, not a relationship, and it is the same call we would make on our own car. If the numbers say to list it yourself, that is exactly what Sam will tell you.
