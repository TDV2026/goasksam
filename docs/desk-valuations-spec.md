# Desk Valuations spec (Nov 3 2026, approved by Sam)

What it is. A login tool at goasksam.com/desk where a valuation expert turns an inbound submission into a signed, evidenced valuation report in about a minute. First customer Hagerty Marketplace, three experts, each their own seat. Marketplace only; not Broad Arrow, nothing insurance. The report is Marketplace's reply to a seller who wants to list: a reserve or a range, and the proof behind it. Built for any platform or house with the same job.

Intake by email. Each org gets one inbound address (hagerty@desk.goasksam.com). Each expert forwards their own submissions. Accept only from a seat of the org. Find the VIN (17 chars, check digit) or year make model; find mileage, gearbox, colour where stated; take image attachments (HEIC converted, resized, upright), keep Drive or Dropbox links unopened; take the consignor name and email from the earliest message in the chain; run the engine read in the background; create the queue row owned by the forwarding seat, status New, original email kept. Edge cases: two VINs, first becomes the sheet and a More VINs flag lets the expert split; pre 1981 chassis, No VIN found box; forward chains parsed whole; a later email on the same thread or VIN adds photos to the existing sheet; no car at all, row created as Needs the car, never dropped. The Desk also takes a VIN or year make model typed at the top.

The queue and the rail. Dark rail, light workspace (the Desk's design). Rail holds Today's queue (car, status New / In progress / Finalised / Sent / Needs the car, owner, time in), My sheets, Company (searchable by VIN, car, expert, date, status), org and seat at the bottom. Several sheets open at once; an open sheet keeps its state. A sheet belongs to the forwarding seat; any seat can pick up an unopened New sheet; once opened, others see it read only; nobody finalises another seat's sheet. Analyst question mode stays in the rail; a question can be pinned to a sheet and prints on the report.

The sheet. One page, seven sections, fixed order. Sections 1 to 4 from the engine, identical whoever runs it. Sections 5 and 6 are the expert's.
1. The car: VIN decode or typed; mileage from the submission or asked for; confirm or correct with one click; prior listing pre-fills colour, gearbox, mileage at last sale.
2. This exact car: every auction appearance of the VIN or chassis, date, venue, sold price or high bid not met, link; or the line No prior auction record for this VIN. Never hidden.
3. The market: median, p25 to p75, count, newest sale, window (12 months, 36 fallback, stated); splits where they price apart (mileage band, gearbox, body, generation); live listings now with venue, bid, end time.
4. Condition: the expert clicks a tier (1 concours, 2 excellent, 3 good, 4 fair) from the photos; comparables re-filter to the same tier with the count shown.
5. What we cannot see: fixed checklist, each item Seen / Not seen / Not supplied with a free line: title status, service records, ownership history, modifications (stock, period, modified, restomod), accident or repair history, provenance. No numbers in this section.
6. Decision: reserve or range (chosen per sheet, one required), one line of reasoning, optional note to the seller. Venue choice is an org setting, off for Hagerty.
7. Finalise: locks the sheet, stamps seat and time, builds the PDF, opens Send.
Every figure comes from the same oneEngineAnswer call the consumer pages use.

Condition tier. Every sale in the record carries a tier assigned on the backend from its own listing text and fields (mileage against the model's bands, owner count, service records mentioned, accident or repair mentioned, restoration mentioned, modification mentioned, title status), stored with the evidence lines that produced it. Too little to tier: Untiered, stays in the wider pool, out of the same tier pool. Fewer than five same tier sales: Too few sales in this condition, wider pool shown, no starting figure. No photo scoring.

The one number. The engine never outputs a reserve. It outputs the range and a starting figure: the median of the comparables after tier and mileage band. Median, never an average. The expert gives a reserve or a range (low and high); fields empty, starting figure and market range beside them. Inside the market range, no reason needed. Outside it: at least one reason ticked (Documentation; Modifications or non original parts; Condition better or worse than the tier on close inspection; Provenance or history; Recent market movement not yet in the sales; Other, typed) plus a typed line. Reasons print on the report in this order: market range, starting figure, reserve or range given, reasons. Note to the seller: a separate note, its own page and in the email, under the org's name, never changes a figure. Too few sales: no starting figure, wider range shown, expert types with a reason. Guards (never block, always require a typed line): same VIN valued before by this org with a difference over the org threshold (default 15%); reserve or range midpoint over the threshold from the starting figure.

Photos and documents. From the submission only; never from any platform into a client report. Thumbnails on the sheet; the expert ticks which go in the report and sets the hero. Documents listed by name, open in a new tab, listed on the report as Documents supplied, never reproduced. Storage per org with RLS; originals kept, resized copies used; deleted with the sheet or on org request.

Finalise, PDF, send. Finalise freezes the engine read, stamps seat and time, immutable after; corrections are a new sheet referencing the old. Finalise refused while a required reason or line is missing, and says which. PDF order: cover (org logo from org settings at the top, car, hero photo, reserve or range, date; the org is the author, no expert's name anywhere), the car, this exact car, the market, condition, comparables (same tier pool, capped, newest first, with date venue price mileage tier link), what we cannot see, decision with reasons, note to the seller where written, photos selected, documents supplied, footer on every page (org, date, report number, methodology line, watermark; seat in the watermark data only). Watermarked per org and seat; counts against the org's monthly export cap. Send: consignor address pre-filled, org's standard message the expert may edit, the note, the PDF attached; sent through the org's own email provider as them; sheet moves to Sent with time and address logged and a copy of what went out kept. Automatic sending off unless the org turns it on.

Saving per seat and org. Every sheet is a private row owned by the org with the seat stamped, on desk_orgs, desk_seats, org_id, RLS on, grants revoked from anon and authenticated. Views: My sheets, Company, This car (earlier sheets for the same VIN beside the new read). Retention: licence plus ninety days, earlier deletion logged.

Rules. Pages and emails compute nothing; every figure from oneEngineAnswer. The engine never outputs a reserve. No starting figure under five same tier sales; no invented adjustment for what the engine cannot observe. Every venue shown the same way in the figures. The archive is never exported; reports capped and watermarked; no bulk export, no API in v1. Photos and documents only from the submission. Every new table and bucket ships with RLS on. The seven sections and the ten report parts are fixed; an org can set venue choice on or off, threshold, branding, sending address and standard message, nothing else in v1. A finalised sheet is immutable.

Build order. 1 Lane B: condition tier on every sale, tier filter and same tier count through oneEngineAnswer, starting figure as an engine field. 2 Lane B: one sheet read call returning sections 1 to 4 for a VIN or year make model plus mileage, frozen read serialisable. 3 Lane D: inbound email and the Send path. 4 Lane E: sheet screen, queue and rail, ownership, checklist and decision with reason rules and guards, finalise and immutability. 5 Lane E: the PDF. 6 Lane S: storage buckets, retention and deletion, sending address verification, seat and org admin for three experts. 7 Lane C: integration and pushes per stage.
