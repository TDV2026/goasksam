Sam Desk — screen redesign (v2, mark-ups applied)

Ask the market a question, get the answer immediately, see just enough to understand it, click anything to prove it, keep talking. One voice, first person. The reading is one plain sentence. One chart at most. The chat box is the only control.

This revision folds in the seven mark-ups: (1) first-person voice; (2) Case 5 loses its table; (3) every figure opens an evidence drawer (Case 1 shows one open) with exclusions labelled by our method vs by your query, and a ranking row opens that car's sales; (4) charts give up their data on hover, nothing at rest; (5) a one-line provenance footer opens the methodology page; (6) the left rail is working memory (New, Recent, Saved, Agents), persisted per org and seat; (7) the mode is a plain Analyst/Leads switch with Leads hidden until built. Numbers are representative archive figures (hammer, premiums backed out; medians and quartiles). Mock-up only.
