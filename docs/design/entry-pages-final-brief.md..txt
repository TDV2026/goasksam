# GoAskSam: Final Design Brief for Find, Market Check and Sell

## Objective

Use the three attached reference images to implement the final design of the **Find, Market Check and Sell entry pages**.

We have spent considerable time refining these designs. The three mockups represent the visual direction we now want.

The priority is a premium, beautifully simple experience that encourages people to search immediately while providing just enough evidence of GoAskSam's capabilities.

**These are entry pages, not results pages.**

Do not redesign the existing Find, Market Check or Sell results experiences. Their current functionality, evidence, charts, recommendations, interactions and data must remain intact.

The screenshots are design references, not sources of verified auction data. Use real product data and photographs in the implementation.

---

## 1. Shared design across all three pages

All three pages should have the same visual identity and structure.

### Header

Retain the existing approved global header:

- Dark forest-green background.
- GoAskSam logo, with small Beta badge, aligned left.
- Find, Market Check, Sell, Tasks and Account navigation aligned right.
- Cream pill highlighting the active page.
- Identical height, typography and positioning across every page.

### Left rail

All three pages must retain the shared left workspace rail.

The screenshots do not all show the rail consistently. The actual implementation must.

Use:

- **Find:** + New search
- **Market Check:** + New check
- **Sell:** + New analysis

Display the relevant recent searches or analyses when they exist, following the existing shared-rail behavior.

Do not repeat the main navigation in the rail.

### Main content

Use a consistent content width, horizontal alignment and vertical rhythm.

Each page should have:

1. A centered headline.
2. One short supporting sentence.
3. One prominent search input.
4. Three natural-language example prompts.
5. One visual example of what GoAskSam delivers.
6. The shared footer.

The search bar must remain the most important interaction.

Use generous white space, but avoid unnecessary empty areas.

### Typography and colors

Match the screenshots:

- Warm cream background.
- Deep green serif headlines.
- Clean sans-serif supporting text.
- Dark-green primary actions.
- Subtle pale-green backgrounds for evidence highlights.
- Thin borders and restrained shadows.
- High-quality real automotive photography.

Avoid decorative icons, excessive labels, giant cards, complicated dashboards or additional marketing copy.

### Search and voice

All three pages must support natural-language searches by typing or speaking.

Use the same search component everywhere.

- Search icon on the left.
- Placeholder text appropriate to the page.
- Microphone clearly visible.
- Dark-green submit arrow on the right.
- Pressing Enter submits.
- Voice transcription populates the same input.
- All existing clarification and search functionality must remain.

The three example prompts must be clickable and execute a real search.

---

# 2. FIND PAGE

Reference: **GoAskSam Find: Live Classic Cars.png**

## Purpose

Find is about discovering live vehicles across multiple auction platforms.

But simply finding cars is not the complete story.

GoAskSam also brings information about those cars, including previous auction appearances, previous completed selling prices and comparable-sales evidence.

We want visitors to understand both benefits without having to read paragraphs about them.

## Hero

Headline:

**What car are you looking for?**

Supporting sentence:

**Search by voice or in natural language.**

Keep this extremely short.

Do not add another paragraph explaining the technology or auction aggregation.

## Search box

Placeholder:

**Speak or type**

Keep the microphone prominently visible, as in the reference.

## Example searches

Use three examples, such as:

- 911 under $40k
- '90s Range Rover
- Aston DB9

The purpose is to show that people can search naturally.

Examples can evolve, but keep them short and varied.

## Featured section

Below the search area, display:

**Live now**

Use the image's three-card arrangement.

Three real vehicles currently at auction, preferably from different auction platforms.

### Each card contains

- Genuine listing photograph.
- Year, make and model.
- Actual auction venue.
- Auction timing or active status.
- One concise piece of market intelligence.

The intelligence should be the distinctive part of the card.

### Three possible intelligence states

**Sold before**

Example display:

"Sold before · $46,000"

Only use this when GoAskSam has verified a previous completed auction sale of the same vehicle.

The amount must be the actual recorded sale price.

Clicking should lead to the previous-sale history or the existing relevant result interaction.

**Market range**

Example display:

"Market range · $20.5k to $29k"

Only show this when sufficient relevant completed-sales evidence exists.

The range must come from the current Market Check engine, not a manually supplied number.

**Listed before**

Example display:

"Listed before · Oct 2023"

This identifies a previous recorded auction appearance, not necessarily a sale.

Do not imply that a car sold when it was merely listed or failed to meet reserve.

### Featured vehicle selection

- Select actual active car auctions.
- Exclude motorcycles, parts and unrelated categories from the featured examples.
- Prefer diverse makes and auction venues.
- Prefer listings with genuine photographs.
- Prefer cars with interesting verified evidence.
- Refresh the selection daily, with checks to remove ended auctions promptly.
- Never invent a previous sale or historical listing to fill an intelligence slot.

When an intelligence type is unavailable, display another verified insight or use a simpler listing card.

### Design

Retain the screenshot's strong photographic presentation.

The intelligence appears in a slim pale-green section at the bottom of each card.

Keep the cards compact and visually balanced.

The three cars should generate interest, but the search box must remain the primary focus.

Do not include the full comparable-sales panel, historical charts or additional filters on this entry page.

# 3. MARKET CHECK PAGE

Reference: **GoAskSam Market Check Dashboard (4).png**

## Purpose

Market Check helps visitors understand what comparable vehicles have actually sold for.

The most important distinction is that GoAskSam shows real completed transactions and lets users inspect the underlying evidence.

This page should feel approachable, not like a financial analytics dashboard.

## Hero

Eyebrow:

**MARKET CHECK**

Headline:

**What have cars like yours sold for?**

Supporting sentence:

**Real completed sales, so you can see what the market is actually paying.**

Keep this one sentence only.

## Search box

Placeholder:

**Tell Sam the car you want to check...**

The microphone remains available.

## Example searches

- 2001 BMW M3
- 997 Carrera S
- E63 AMG

The examples must execute actual Market Check searches.

## Featured Market Check

Below the search area, show one elegant horizontal example card.

Use the exact general composition of the attached reference:

**Left side:** Large vehicle photograph.

**Right side:** Market Check summary.

### Example structure

Eyebrow:

**EXAMPLE MARKET CHECK**

Vehicle:

**2001 BMW M3**

Primary figure:

**$30,000 to $36,500**

Supporting evidence:

**11 completed sales · Last 12 months**

Then a pale-green evidence row showing one actual completed transaction:

**A recent sale: $33,000**

Cars & Bids · 72,100 miles

These numbers are illustrative from the mockup and must not be hardcoded as verified results.

The implementation must use an actual Market Check example with real completed transactions, a genuine photograph and the correct analysis period.

### Why this layout works

It communicates three things immediately:

1. GoAskSam provides a completed-sales range.
2. The result is supported by actual transactions.
3. Users can inspect those transactions.

This is enough to encourage someone to conduct their own Market Check.

### Interaction

Make the example card clickable.

It should open the real Market Check results for the featured vehicle.

The highlighted individual transaction should also open its original auction receipt where appropriate.

### Important design restrictions

Do not include on the entry page:

- Historical market charts.
- Mileage refinement inputs.
- Body-style and transmission filters.
- Large transaction tables.
- Median and quartile breakdowns.
- Multiple competing market figures.

Those features belong in the actual Market Check results.

### Data selection

Select the featured market example from existing verified results.

Prefer a reasonably well-defined vehicle population with a meaningful, relatively focused range.

Do not artificially narrow a range just to make the preview appear more accurate.

The stated number of sales, dates and range must describe the same evidence population.

The preview can refresh daily.

# 4. SELL PAGE

Reference: **GoAskSam Porsche Selling Analysis(1).png**

## Purpose

Sell should demonstrate that GoAskSam does much more than point someone toward an auction website.

It uses completed-sales evidence to help answer:

- Where should I sell?
- What does the historical auction-day evidence show?
- What does the evidence say about reserve versus no reserve?

The most important output is an actionable selling recommendation supported by real auction results.

## Hero

Eyebrow:

**SELL**

Headline:

**Where should you sell your car?**

Supporting sentence:

**Get a tailored recommendation based on real sales, platform performance and market conditions.**

Do not add more explanatory copy.

## Search box

Placeholder:

**Tell Sam the car you want to sell...**

Keep the same voice and text capabilities as Find and Market Check.

## Example prompts

- Sell my 997 Carrera S
- Best time to sell a Defender
- Should I use a reserve?

These should initiate the existing Sell query flow.

## Featured Sell analysis

Use the reference screenshot's structure:

A single wide card with a photograph on the left and the recommendation on the right.

### Left side

Large photograph of the relevant vehicle.

### Right side

Eyebrow:

**EXAMPLE SELL ANALYSIS**

Vehicle:

**2004 Porsche 911 Carrera**

Then three clear pieces of intelligence.

### A. Recommended platform

Label:

**SAM'S PLATFORM PICK**

Primary answer:

**Bring a Trailer**

Supporting evidence:

"11 of 14 relevant completed sales were on BaT."

This is the type of evidence we want to display. Use the real current figures from the existing Sell engine.

The recommendation must come from the actual engine, not a hardcoded choice of platform.

### B. Auction day

Label:

**AUCTION DAY**

Example:

**Wednesday**

Supporting information:

"5 of 14 sales ended Wednesday."

Important: The system must distinguish the most frequently observed selling day from a proven best day.

If the evidence only supports which day appeared most often, describe it as an observation rather than claiming that sellers achieve better prices that day.

### C. Reserve strategy

Label:

**RESERVE STRATEGY**

Example:

**With reserve?**

Supporting text:

"Explore how reserve and no-reserve listings performed."

We want to demonstrate that GoAskSam can examine this question.

Do not recommend a reserve purely because reserve auctions are more numerous or have higher observed prices.

Use the existing supported Sell evidence. Where it supports only an observation or comparison, state that clearly.

### Bottom action

One discreet text link:

**Explore the full selling analysis**

The complete card can be clickable, with the link providing an obvious action.

No giant green CTA on the landing page.

No additional alternative-platform logos.

No generic platform ranking.

## Design

Match the reference:

- Large, attractive vehicle photo on the left.
- Vehicle heading and recommended platform on the right.
- Two compact intelligence sections below.
- Small footer action inside the card.
- Clean spacing and subtle borders.

This provides considerably more value than simply saying Sam recommends Bring a Trailer, without overwhelming the visitor.

# 5. CONSISTENCY ACROSS THE THREE PAGES

These are three separate capabilities within one GoAskSam product.

The user should recognize the design immediately when switching between them.

### Shared elements must match

- Header height and logo position.
- Left rail width and positioning.
- Content max-width and gutters.
- Headline typography.
- Supporting-text typography.
- Search box dimensions.
- Microphone and arrow styling.
- Example-chip styling.
- Spacing above the featured section.
- Footer appearance.
- Mobile responsive behavior.

### The featured examples should differ

**Find:** Three live listing cards, each with a verified intelligence insight.

**Market Check:** One completed-sales range, one real transaction and a vehicle photograph.

**Sell:** One selling recommendation with platform, auction-day and reserve-strategy evidence.

Do not force identical card structures across all three pages.

They should share the same design language, but communicate different product benefits.

### Do not add unnecessary sections

We do not need:

- How it works steps.
- Long marketing explanations.
- Testimonials.
- Extra feature grids.
- Charts on entry pages.
- Multiple competing CTAs.

The pages should feel calm, premium and highly focused.

---

# 6. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

The screenshots are static visual references, but the finished product must be fully functional.

### Search

Every search input and example prompt must work using the existing search infrastructure.

### Featured data

All featured vehicles, evidence and recommendations must be grounded in actual GoAskSam records.

No invented prices, auction histories, dates, recommendation statistics or active listings.

### Navigation

Featured examples must open the relevant results page, preserving the vehicle query and context.

### Results pages

Do not modify the existing Find, Market Check or Sell results layouts or product logic in this task.

Maintain their existing search refinements, historical transaction links, charts, recommendations, PowerSeller features and supporting evidence.

### Responsive layout

At desktop widths, use the full photographic arrangements shown in the references.

At tablet widths, preserve readability without excessively shrinking the featured cards.

On mobile:

- Stack the featured cards or card columns.
- Keep the search input prominent.
- Make the microphone easily accessible.
- Prevent clipping of prices, headlines and evidence.
- Maintain the approved mobile navigation.

### Performance

Do not allow large featured photographs to slow down search interaction.

Use appropriately sized images and sensible caching.

The search input should be interactive without waiting for featured examples to finish loading.

1. Use the three attached images as the visual references.
2. Reuse the existing shared header, workspace rail, footer and search components.
3. Implement the three entry-page layouts.
4. Connect featured examples to real data and existing routes.
5. Ensure historical sales and other intelligence labels are accurate.

7. Verify desktop and mobile layouts.

## Final instruction

**The main objective is to get people searching.**

The featured examples are demonstrations of what they can discover, not separate dashboards.

Find should make searching across auction platforms feel effortless.

Market Check should make completed-sales evidence feel accessible.

Sell should demonstrate that the same evidence can inform a real selling decision.

Keep the experience premium, simple and consistent, while preserving the depth of the existing results pages.

Do not rebuild the rest of GoAskSam to implement these three designs.
