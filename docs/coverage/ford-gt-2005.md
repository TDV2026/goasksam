A typed "2006 Ford GT Heritage Edition" gives a pool of 12 sales (span $640,000 to $2,105,000), more than the 7 Heritage sales we hold in the window, so it is not a strict Heritage-only read.

**Finding for Lane B or D (no code touched here):** 3 genuine Ford GTs (2 Heritage Editions on Bring a Trailer, 1 plain 2006 at RM Sotheby's) carry the project flag "tribute" and are set aside as "project or incomplete car (tribute)".
