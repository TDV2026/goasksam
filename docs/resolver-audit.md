# Resolver audit

Generated by `node scripts/resolverAudit.mjs --write` (offline, no vendor request, about 37 seconds; too slow for prePush, so the pinned cases live in scripts/resolverLanesCheck.mjs).

**Size:** 274 makes and models the resolver knows (lib/vehicleData.js aliases, ownership, production rules, curated models; lib/generations.js; plus the named launch cars), 8359 queries typed the ways a person types them: year make model, make model, lowercase, model alone, spacing and hyphen variants (SRT-10 / SRT10 / SRT 10, C63 / C 63, RX-7 / RX7), nicknames, curated trims, and the body, edition, variant, series and wheelbase words added in earlier jobs. Each query runs through resolveVehicle (Market Check and Sell) and through parseQuery then resolveForBuy (Buy and Tasks). The resolver reads makes and models from the audit list itself (a stand-in for the seeded taxonomy), so a flag means the typed words were read wrongly, not that a model was unknown.

**Checked:** the two paths differ; a known car comes back with a null make or model, or a different one; a typed word is carried nowhere (trim, trimNumber, edition, variant, series, wheelbase, body); a model is wrongly cased; a number is read as a year. Misspellings (they confirm) and names two makes share (NSX is an Acura and a Honda) are skipped by design.

**Result now:** 30 flags on 13 queries. Every one left sits in Lane B's lib/live/search.js (listed under FOR LANE B in docs/lane-notes.md) or is a known case below.

## Known cases left in the resolver's own files

- Dodge Charger Shelby: reads as the Shelby make (Cobra); the 1983 Dodge Shelby Charger is a Dodge. Not fixed: needs a make-collision rule.
- Ford F-150 Raptor: the model is "F-150 Raptor" typed whole, but F-150 with trim Raptor when "Raptor" is typed alone. Same car, two shapes. Not fixed: changing the shape moves the comp pool.
- Mazda MX-5 Club: the trim word Club is not in the locked trim vocabulary, so it is not carried.
- 550 Spyder typed alone: asks for a make (Porsche 550 Spyder or Ferrari 550); by design while two makes use 550.
- Bentley 4.5 litre blower typed as a nickname: the audit flags the digits of the alias; the car resolves correctly.
- Range Rover Sport / Discovery Sport plus the edition word Sport Classic: the model already holds the word Sport; audit artifact.
