GoAskSam for business

GoAskSam for business is a reference service built the same way the consumer product is: entirely from real completed sales, never an opinion. For a given vehicle we return a transaction-derived reference range with the number of sales behind it, the window those sales fall in, and the individual receipts attached, so every figure traces back to a sale that actually happened and can be checked.

Who it is for

  • Insurers setting cover on collector and enthusiast vehicles.
  • Lenders who need a defensible market reference on an asset.
  • Dealers who want the recent sales record behind a number, not a guess.

The output is a market reference grounded in receipts, meant to be shown to your own customers and auditors. It is not an estimate and it is not our opinion of a car; it is what comparable cars have actually sold for, with the evidence attached.
